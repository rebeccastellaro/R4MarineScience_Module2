Wrangle final dataset

At this point the dataset only contains rows where a species was actually caught, meaning that if a species was not seen at a site on a given day, there is simply no row for it. This is a problem, since “not caught” and “not recorded” are not the same thing. The missing site, date and species combinations need to be filled in with explicit zeros before any proper statistics can be run.

At this stage I can start thinking properly about hypotheses and what might be driving the ecology of these species in this system.

Before going further, I want to confirm there is an actual, meaningful difference in the environmental variables across zones. There is little point examining fish patterns against salinity if salinity does not vary much across the estuary.

Salinity

There is clearly a real difference in salinity across zones. Existing research has looked at how some of these species respond to salinity gradients, which is a good indication this is worth investigating further.

I will also check the other two environmental variables before deciding what to focus on.

Turbidity

Temperature

I will continue with salinity as the main environmental variable of interest, though there are clearly differences in turbidity and temperature across zones as well, which could also be influencing fish abundance patterns. These are worth flagging as potential confounding factors during interpretation, rather than treating salinity as the only relevant variable.
